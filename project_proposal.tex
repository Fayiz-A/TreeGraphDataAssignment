\section*{Problem Description and Research Question}
Transportation systems rely on interconnected highways, roads, bridges, and tunnels. These networks are always vulnerable to construction closures, accidents, and natural disasters, which could affect the connectivity of the road network. Each year, numerous cities are threatened by natural disasters that could harm significant routes. When these routes become unusable, people may not have access to fundamental necessities, such as hospitals and food providers. Therefore, it is important to study transportation networks and dependent routes to prevent an area from being isolated from the rest of its city during emergencies. This motivates us to have a deeper understanding of road networks and how to use technology and computer science to solve this problem.
The state of Pennsylvania in the United States involves multiple urban and rural areas. It has a diverse topography of mountains and rivers that make constraints on road construction. Over the years, the state has experienced severe storms, floods, and hurricanes that caused infrastructure destruction or temporary closure of roads. For example, in April 2025, Pennsylvania faced a thunderstorm that took trees down on the roads, blocking the way to several sites (US). \textbf{ The goal of this project is to study how resilient Pennsylvania’s road network is to closures to determine the most crucial intersections.}
